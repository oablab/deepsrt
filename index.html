FAQ

Do I need my own API key?

Yes. DeepSRT uses your Google Gemini key or a supported provider. Gemini keys are available from Google AI Studio. Requests go directly from your device to that provider. Provider usage may be billed to you; set a monthly spending cap before heavy use.

Is DeepSRT a subscription?

No. DeepSRT PRO and DeepSRT Mobile are separate one-time App Store purchases. Neither app has a DeepSRT subscription.

Which app should I choose?

Choose DeepSRT PRO for the full Mac experience, including MCP tools for AI agents. Choose DeepSRT Mobile for live captions, summaries, and transcripts designed for iPhone and iPad.

Does my viewing data pass through DeepSRT servers?

Never — there are no DeepSRT servers. The app fetches captions directly from YouTube and sends AI requests directly to the provider you configure. No account, analytics, or middleman.

Which languages are supported?

The apps support Traditional and Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, and Thai. Transcript availability depends on the video; live translation fills the language gap.

Does it work with members-only or login-required videos?

No. Members-only, private, and other login-gated videos reject the official embedded player. This is a YouTube restriction shared by all third-party apps. Public and unlisted videos with a link work.

Where can I get the apps?

DeepSRT PRO is on the Mac App Store. DeepSRT Mobile is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Both are one-time purchases.
