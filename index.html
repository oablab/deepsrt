FAQ

Do I need my own API key?

Yes — DeepSRT is powered by your own Google Gemini API key, which you can create for free at Google AI Studio. Because the key is yours, there are no usage fees to DeepSRT, no monthly AI subscription, and your requests go directly to Google.

Is DeepSRT a subscription?

No. DeepSRT is a one-time purchase on the Mac App Store. The Chrome extension companion is free.

Does my viewing data pass through DeepSRT's servers?

Never — there are no DeepSRT servers. The app fetches captions directly from YouTube and sends transcript text directly to Google's Gemini API using your key. No account, no analytics, no middleman.

Which languages are supported?

Summaries and title translations are available in 繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Français, and ไทย. Transcripts follow the caption languages available on the video.

Do I need Chrome to use it?

No. The Mac app is fully standalone — paste a link, get a summary, transcript, and built-in player. The Chrome extension is an optional companion for people who like summaries in the browser side panel while they watch.

What happened to DeepSRT v1?

DeepSRT v1 ran in the cloud. v2 is a ground-up rebuild that moves everything onto your Mac: faster, more private, and no longer dependent on shared infrastructure. The extension lives on — it now talks to the app instead of the cloud.
