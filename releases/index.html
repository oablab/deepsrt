Releases

What each version of DeepSRT for Mac brought, newest first.

  • 1.5.1LatestAugust 10, 2026
    Fixed a caption bug: a video whose native track YouTube labels as plain "zh" (typically Simplified) was being treated as an exact match for a Traditional Chinese preference, so translation was skipped and Simplified captions showed up for users who asked for 繁體中文.
  • 1.5.0August 9, 2026
    DeepSRT PRO: the app takes its name from the family it's now part of (PRO, Mobile, News). Adds caption styling, a real history panel, captions that duck out of the way on click, and localized error messages. Read the note.
  • 1.4.1August 9, 2026
    Superseded same-day by 1.5.0 — the rename plus four features didn't fit a patch version.
  • 1.4.0August 8, 2026
    AI transcription for videos with no captions at all — opt-in, one ingestion pass instead of three, and resilient to a bad time window. Read the note.
  • 1.3.0August 7, 2026
    Built-in MCP endpoint: six tools (search, transcript, summary, channel listing, video info, open-in-app) so AI agents and CLIs can work with what you're watching — your own key, nothing leaving your Mac. Read the note.
  • 1.2.0August 5, 2026
    Universal binary (Apple silicon + Intel), a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint as a per-task AI provider (OpenCode Zen, OpenRouter, DeepSeek presets), per-task Gemini model selection, and Traditional Chinese split into Taiwan and Hong Kong variants.
  • 1.1.0August 4, 2026
    The free Safari extension ships on the Mac App Store: a "Play in DeepSRT" context menu and toolbar hand-off with playback position. In-app UI now follows your content-language setting across 7 locales.
  • 1.0.1August 4, 2026
    Summary and transcript panes default to the right of the video.
  • 1.0.0July 31, 2026
    First release on the Mac App Store. AI summaries and clickable transcripts for YouTube, powered by your own Gemini key, with the spine+annotation architecture that keeps bilingual captions in sync. Read the note.
